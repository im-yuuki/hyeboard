## Design Context

### Users
University students, primarily VNU/UET, using lookup tools on desktop and mobile to reconcile course IDs, student identifiers, grades/transcripts, and batches.

### Job
Resolve exact academic identifiers and records quickly without ambiguity; inspect progress/errors during potentially slow upstream work.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, trustworthy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Calm academic utility; balanced density; restrained university accent; data-first, not decorative. Both existing light/dark/UET themes must work.

### Design Principles
- Clear hierarchy.
- Progressive disclosure.
- Compact scannable records.
- Honest async/error states.
- Full mobile capability.
